Spot Scorer.

Pick a spot. Get its color and audio metrics, the sector defaults it lives inside, and three to five numerical targets it would need to hit to read as distinctive. 47 spots, 13 brands.

By Barbora Šandová. Source on GitHub.

Sector context

Each gauge shows where this spot sits inside its sector's distribution. The shaded band is the inter-quartile range (p25 to p75); the dashed line is the median. A dot near the edge of the band means the spot is already pushing toward distinctive; a dot in the middle means it lives at the category default.

04

Brief targets

If the goal is a distinctive spot in this category, these are the numerical moves. Each target takes the spot OUT of the inter-quartile box on a single axis. Pick one or two; trying to move all of them at once defeats the focus.
